> [!abstract] Integral 17 - 260630
> Assume that $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ is even function and $\pi$-periodic, that is, $f(x)=f(x+\pi)=f(-x)$ holds for all real $x$. Then prove following two formula using partial fraction expansion of cotangent, provided in Answer of IOTW \#15:
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \int_0^\infty\frac{\sin^2x}{x^2}f(x)dx &= \int_0^\infty\frac{\sin x}{x}f(x)=\int_0^\frac\pi2f(x)dx.&&(1) \\
> \int_0^\infty\frac{\sin^4x}{x^4}f(x)dx &= \int_0^\frac\pi2f(x)dx-\frac23\int_0^\frac\pi2\sin^2xf(x)dx. && (2)
> \end{aligned}
> $$
> And use these results to solve IOTW \#14 and \#16 again.
> 
> What kind of formula similar to the above would be needed to solve IOTW \#15?

> [!abstract] Integral 25 - 260825

> Let two integral $I,J$ as:
> $$
> I:=\int_0^\infty\frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^3+\phi x+1}},\qquad J:=\int_\rho^\infty\frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^3+\phi x+1}}.
> $$
> 
> State whether $I/J$ is a rational, and prove it.
