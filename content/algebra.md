# Algebra

This is a test page for mathematical content.

## 1. Definition

> [!note] Definition (Group)
> A **group** is a set $G$ together with a binary operation satisfying the group axioms.

## 2. Theorem

> [!important] Theorem (Lagrange)
> Let $G$ be a finite group and $H$ a subgroup.
> Then
>
> $$
> |H| \mid |G|.
> $$

## 3. Proof

By considering the left cosets of $H$ in $G$, we obtain

$$
|G| = [G:H]|H|.
$$

This proves the theorem.
